Hamleys, Share A Smile

Personal Project | Brand & Campaign Reimagining

The Brief

The brief was to take an existing brand and reimagine it for a completely new target audience and market segment, rethinking its positioning, identity, and communication.

The Idea

I chose Hamleys and reimagined it as Hamleys Smile, shifting the focus from premium toys to affordable, accessible play for families in Tier 2 and emerging Tier 3 cities. The idea was simple: play should never feel out of reach. So, I reimagined Hamleys as Hamleys Smile, a more accessible, inclusive toy brand, and built its identity, packaging, brand system, and “Share A Smile” campaign around one simple belief: Where Smiles Come to Play.

The Process

I started with the new positioning and audience, then built the identity around the question: what should an affordable toy brand feel like to a child? From there, I explored the logo, colour system, typography, illustrations, characters, patterns, photography style, and applications, building each element around the same ideas of accessibility, play, warmth, and imagination. The final system was brought together through a complete set of brand guidelines.

Visual Identity

The identity combines a hand-drawn, rounded wordmark inspired by children’s drawings, with a curved “smile” and stars symbolising joy, imagination, and wonder. A vibrant palette of red, yellow, pink, blue, and green brings energy, warmth, and a cheerful personality across every touchpoint.

Illustration, Drawn By My Cousin

Instead of creating polished illustrations myself, I gave my younger cousin complete freedom to draw whatever came naturally to him. The result was a collection of imperfect drawings that became part of the Hamleys Smile. The rawness was intentional: it keeps the identity rooted in genuine childhood imagination.

Packaging

The packaging extends the same idea of letting children be creative. The front uses a playful scene inspired by the kind of drawings children naturally make. The back is intentionally left blank, giving children a space to draw, doodle, and make the packaging their own.

Brand Collaterals

The identity was extended across business cards, loyalty cards, and the website, creating a consistent and recognisable brand experience across every interaction.

Brand Guidelines

The full identity was documented in a brand guidelines deck covering logo usage, colour, typography, and every brand element, keeping future applications consistent.

Campaign, Share A Smile

The rebrand was taken one step further through a campaign built as a four-day Children’s Week activation, from 11 to 14 November, with the same belief: every child deserves the chance to play. Share A Smile turns the brand’s positioning into a real community experience, bringing children from different backgrounds together to draw, play, exchange, and share.

The Process

The campaign transforms the spaces around Hamleys Smile into places for creativity and community. It brings together three key initiatives: DRAW, turning streets into giant doodle zones; EXCHANGE, bringing a pre-loved toy and exchanging it for a new one; and GIVE, where collected toys are cleaned, repaired, and gifted to children who need them.

Campaign Visual Language

I created a hand-drawn campaign logo with a chalk-like smiley, surrounded by stars, connecting directly to the street-doodle concept. The campaign palette expanded into yellow, orange, green, and soft blush, giving the communication a brighter, outdoor and community-led character.

Campaign Collaterals

I translated the campaign across multiple touchpoints: social media, flyers, posters, billboards, and outdoor applications.
