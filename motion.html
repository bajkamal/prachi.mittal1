Toblerone — Reimagining the Pack

A six-piece chocolate “vault” reimagined for kids — playful, collectible, and worth keeping.

The Brief

Reimagine an existing brand’s packaging for a new target audience, while keeping its core identity recognisable.

03

The Idea

I reimagined Toblerone for kids as a six-piece chocolate “vault” that feels playful, collectible and worth keeping. The hexagonal box doubles as a storage box once the chocolates are gone.

04

Building The Identity

The blue-to-pink palette creates a playful mix of both worlds, while the six alternating packs add a collectible feel. The polar bear in a snowy, illustrated environment connects back to Toblerone’s mountain identity while making the brand more playful and relatable to kids.

05

Bringing The Brand To Life

“What Will You Store Here?” turns opening the pack into an experience, rather than just revealing chocolate.
